Game Name: Undecided (Data Entry Pharmacy game)

Status: Early Development 

Concept: Player is stationed at a data entry station in a pharmacy and receives 
prescriptions from customers, player has to verify that the prescription is correct,
unforged, the dose wont kill the customer, then the player has to enter the prescription 
and move on to the next customer. At the end of each day the player will get to know
what mistakes they made and there is going to be a scoring system which will deduct points
for making mistakes.

Engine: Unity 6000.6.3f1
